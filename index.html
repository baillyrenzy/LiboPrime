Build a routine you can keep

The men who feel their best usually share a few simple habits. These are general wellness tips, and they work well alongside LiboPrime.

Start with one or two changes, make them routine, then add more. Small steps repeated daily beat big plans you drop in a week.

Write your routine down, keep your capsules where you will see them, and tie them to something you already do every day, like breakfast or dinner.

Add LiboPrime to your routine

Five habits that support everyday vitality

Sleep

Aim for a regular bedtime and seven to nine hours. Sleep shapes energy, mood and recovery.

Movement

Mix strength training with walking or cardio. Even short sessions add up across the week.

Food

Build meals around protein, vegetables, whole grains and healthy fats. Take your capsules with a meal.

Hydration

Drink water through the day, especially before and after exercise.

Stress

Make time to switch off. A walk, time outdoors or a hobby all help.

Check-ups

See your doctor regularly and share any supplements you take.

Limit alcohol

Less alcohol usually means better sleep, better workouts and more energy the next day.

Stay connected

Time with friends and family is good for mood and motivation.

Track progress

Keep a simple note of your sleep, training and energy so you can see what works.
